kabir / BROWSER SETUP

Connect your browser to KDM

  1. Extract the portable KDM app into a permanent folder.
  2. Double-click Install-Browser.cmd in that folder. This registers the native desktop bridge for your Windows account.
  3. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select KDM's browser folder.
  4. Pin the extension. Open its popup and look for Desktop connected.

Automatically download in KDM is on by default. Click a download link normally: the extension pauses the browser transfer and cancels it after KDM saves the queue entry. If the handoff fails, the browser transfer resumes.

Turn the switch off to download with your browser. Browser-local exports and detected POST downloads stay in the browser. Login cookies and request bodies are not transferred; turn capture off for downloads that require your browser session. Transfer failures after KDM accepts a file appear in KDM for retry.

You can also right-click a link and choose Download with KDM, paste a URL into the popup, or select detected direct video/audio.

Updating an existing installation? Reload KDM on your browser's extensions page, and approve the added Manage your downloads permission if prompted.

KDM in the system tray

Closing or minimizing KDM keeps transfers running beside the Windows clock. Use the hidden-icons arrow if the orange KDM icon is not visible. Double-click it, or choose Open KDM in this extension, to restore the window.

Right-click the tray icon for Show KDM, Pause all, Resume all, and Exit KDM. Exit saves download progress before stopping.

If you move the app folder, run Install-Browser.cmd again. Adaptive HLS/DASH manifests can be copied; complete-video assembly is not supported.
